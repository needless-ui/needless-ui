Add Dialog and Menu. `@needless-ui/angular/dialog` wraps the native `<dialog>` element (two-way `[(open)]`, return values, dismissible or not), and `@needless-ui/angular/menu` builds on Angular Aria with popover rendering, viewport-aware positioning, submenus, and checkbox and radio items. Tokens gain shadow support, raised-surface, backdrop and shadow colors, and `@angular/aria` and `@angular/cdk` become peer dependencies.
