Initial release: `nuiButton` for native buttons and links, `nuiDialog` for the native `<dialog>` element, and `nuiMenu` built on Angular Aria, with submenus and checkbox and radio items. Standalone, signal-based directives, with `@angular/core`, `@angular/common`, `@angular/aria` and `@angular/cdk` 22 as peer dependencies.
