A carousel (`@needless-ui/angular/carousel`): slides in a row that scrolls and snaps natively, so swiping, trackpads and the arrow keys move it, with previous and next buttons, dots, several slides per view (`perView`, or `auto` for slides with their own width), `loop`, and `[(index)]`. With `autoplay`, it turns by itself behind a rotation control first in the tab order, with a ring that fills until the next slide; rotation pauses under the pointer and on hidden pages, and stops for good when keyboard focus comes in, as the WAI-ARIA carousel pattern asks. Where it lands is announced after a swipe or a button, and it runs right to left.
